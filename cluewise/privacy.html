Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you play Cluewise ("the game"), published by Mayura Games ("we", "us"), and the choices you have. We have written it to be short and clear.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and the game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can continue where you left off: levels solved, experience points, gold coins, hints, emblems, album stickers, streaks, purchased items, settings (such as language, sound and vibration) and a record of the version of these documents you accepted and when. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising

The game is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device model and operating system, app information, diagnostics and how you interact with ads. In regions where the law requires consent for personalised ads, the game asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (such as gold packs, hint packs, the Monthly Pass and Remove Ads), processed by Google Play. Google Play tells the game that a purchase was made, with an order number, so the item can be delivered; the game keeps that confirmation on your device so an item is never delivered twice and so Remove Ads can be restored. We never receive your name, payment card or bank details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not run servers that receive your game data.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stored on your device is kept until you delete it: uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it permanently. We hold no copy. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can review and delete it with the controls in your Google Account and your device's ad settings. Your purchase history is kept by Google Play in your Google account.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game does not ask any player for personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

Security

Your progress stays on your device, so it is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock and app sandbox. Ads and purchases are handled by Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold personal information about you on our servers; you can delete everything the game stores by uninstalling it or clearing its data. For information processed by Google for advertising, you can use the controls described above or contact Google. You may contact us with any privacy question or request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make a change, we will update the version and date above and ask you to review and accept the new version in the game before you continue playing.

Contact

If you have any questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, please email Mayura Games at cluewisesupport@gmail.com.
